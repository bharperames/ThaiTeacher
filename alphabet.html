Thai Letter Anatomy

Thai has 44 consonants, but far fewer distinct shapes. Most letters are a base shape plus one small part: a tail, a foot, a notch or a flick. Learn the handful of parts, then learn each family as “the base letter, plus this.”

The parts

Head หัว

บ ม อ

The small loop. You start writing a letter at its head. Which side the head is on, and whether it curls in or out, tells many letters apart.

Rising tail

ป ฝ ฟ ฬ

A stroke that climbs above the top of the other letters.

Foot

ฎ ฏ ฐ ญ

A curl that hangs below the line.

Notch

ต ฃ ฅ

A small zigzag cut into the top of the letter.

Flick

ช ซ ศ ส

A short extra stroke at the top, added to an existing letter.

No head

ก

A few letters have no loop at all. ก is the most common one.
